# ColdTrace

Cold-chain operators monitor refrigerated assets, act on deviations and gaps,
and generate traceability reports. This increment implements the nine prioritized
ADD stories with the existing Angular application and Strangler services.
Existing navigation, authentication, branding and remaining course functionality
are preserved. Success means real API-backed state, notifications with deep
links, acknowledge-and-assign with ten-second undo, and period reports.
